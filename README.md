### 02 — NetSupport Manager RAT Investigation

**SOC Tier 1 Simulation | Threat Investigation | Host & User Attribution**

An evidence-driven investigation of suspected NetSupport Manager RAT activity using a publicly available cybersecurity training dataset.

**Investigation highlights:**

- Analyzed 15,512 packets across more than four hours of captured traffic.
- Identified the associated Windows workstation through Ethernet, IPv4, and NBNS evidence.
- Correlated Kerberos and SAMR traffic to establish the associated account identity.
- Investigated NetSupport-compatible HTTP communication over TCP port 443.
- Identified recurring polling behavior and extracted network indicators.
- Reconstructed the incident timeline and documented seven evidence-supported findings.
- Produced a complete Tier 1 incident report with a provisional severity assessment and escalation recommendations.

**Repository:** [netsupport-rat-network-investigation](https://github.com/alirezarahmani-cyber/netsupport-rat-network-investigation)

## Investigation Methodology

My cybersecurity investigations follow a structured, evidence-driven SOC workflow:

    Security Alert & Initial Triage
                   |
                   v
          Host Identification
                   |
                   v
      Network & Protocol Analysis
                   |
                   v
           User Attribution
                   |
                   v
          Threat Investigation
                   |
                   v
      Evidence Correlation & IOCs
                   |
                   v
      Incident Assessment & Reporting

I emphasize the distinction between confirmed observations, suspected malicious activity, and unresolved investigative questions. Findings are documented with supporting evidence, confidence assessments, and clearly stated limitations.
